# RCCG Learning Path

A free, mobile-friendly place to study the RCCG discipleship manuals and take the class tests.

**Open the site:** https://babatundeawo.github.io/rccg-training-portal/

## What you can do

- **Believers' Class** – 8 studies on the foundations of the faith.
- **Baptismal Class** – 15 studies preparing for water baptism.
- **Workers-in-Training** – 33 studies for those preparing to serve.
- **Read with Scripture** – verses open in place as you read.
- **Take the class tests** – random questions, a timer, and a result you can share on WhatsApp.
- **Pick up where you left off** – the site reopens a study at the point you stopped, and shows your study streak and week.
- **Bookmark and take notes** – keep private notes per study and bookmark any point; find them on one page.
- **Sync across devices** – sign in (optional) to carry progress, notes and bookmarks between devices.
- **Share with your class leader** – an optional switch lets approved class leaders see your progress. Notes and bookmarks are never shared.
- **Install it like an app** and **save a track for offline use**.
- **Light and dark themes**, and **press Ctrl/⌘ + K** to jump anywhere.
