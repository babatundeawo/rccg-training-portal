    Good to know

    Questions, answered

    Do I need an account?

    No. Your progress is saved in your browser on this device. If you want it to follow you between your phone, tablet and computer, you can optionally sign in with your email or Google.

    How does the test work?

    Enter your name, date of birth and phone number (Workers-in-Training also asks for your unit or department), then answer a paper of randomly chosen questions in 15 minutes. If time runs out, the test is submitted automatically and unanswered questions count as incorrect. Your result can then be shared to WhatsApp.

    Do I have to finish every study before the test?

    The test is open at any time, but it is written to follow the studies. Each track invites you to take it once you have gone through them all.

    Is my test information stored?

    No. Your name, date of birth and phone number are only used to prepare your result message and are not saved by this site. If you sign in, your email, name and completed studies are saved to your account so they can sync between devices.

    Does it work without internet?

    Pages, studies and scriptures you have already opened can usually be reopened when your connection drops. Signing in and syncing need a connection.
