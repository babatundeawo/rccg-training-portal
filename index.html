Grow at your own pace, one study at a time.

This replaces the printed manuals with a guided, mobile-friendly companion for the Believers' Class, Baptismal Class, and Workers-in-Training. No page to lose, no chapter to skip — just tap in and keep going.

How this works

Pick a track above. Each study reveals one focused idea at a time — read it, tap Continue, and the next one appears in place. Any Bible reference is tappable: it opens the actual verse right there, verse numbers and all, so you never have to go hunting for it. When you're ready, each track has its own test day quiz.
